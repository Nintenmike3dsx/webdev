The Legend of Nano

In a castle deep in the highlands, a band of terriers rose to power. Known as the Nanos, these bold warriors proved that size means nothing when your heart — and your treat pouch — is full.

The kingdom is governed by one law: the strongest deck rules. Summon your Nanos, spend your Treats wisely, and outwit your opponent across three fierce turns.

How to Play

Each player starts with 20 Health Points and a deck of 20 cards. On your turn, you gain Treats (the currency of battle), play cards from your hand, and command your Nanos to attack.

The first player to reduce their opponent's HP to zero wins the kingdom!

  1. Gain Treats: Add Treat cards to your pool to fuel your plays.
  2. Play Cards: Pay a card's Treat cost to summon it.
  3. Battle Phase: Your Characters attack the opponent's field.
  4. End Turn: Draw back up to 5 cards.

The Three Card Types

Every card in the Nano deck belongs to one of three types. A balanced deck uses all three in harmony.

Character

Nanos that fight on the battlefield. They have Attack and Defense values. Protect your own; destroy your opponent's.

Action

Powerful one-time effects: debuffs, redirects, board sweepers. Play at the right moment to turn the tide.

Treat

Resource cards with zero Treat cost. Play them to add Treats to your pool and unlock bigger plays sooner.

Building Your Deck

Your deck must contain exactly 20 cards. Each card has a cardCount limit — Rares are limited to 1 copy, Uncommons to 2, and Commons to 3 or 4.

Head to the Scriptorium to browse all cards and assemble your deck. The balance visualizer shows your treat-cost curve and attack/defense totals so you can tune your strategy.

  • Rare cards: max 1 copy
  • Uncommon cards: max 2 copies
  • Common cards: max 3–4 copies (per card)
  • Total deck size: 20 cards

Reading a Card

Each Character card displays three key numbers:

  • ⚔ ATK Attack — damage dealt when this Character attacks
  • 🛡 DEF Defense — how much damage is needed to destroy it
  • 🦴 COST Treat cost — Treats you must spend to play this card
